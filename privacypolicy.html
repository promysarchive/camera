POLICY

PROMYS is a creative platform exploring photography, archives, expression and connection. This policy explains how information is handled when you visit or use the website.

LAST UPDATED: 2 NOVEMBER 2026

01 / About Promys

PROMYS is a creative platform featuring photography, digital archives and anonymous written responses.

This Privacy Policy explains how information is collected, used, stored and handled when you interact with the website.

02 / Information We Handle

Anonymous responses

When you submit a response through PROMYS, the text of your answer is stored in a Supabase database. Submissions are reviewed by a human for moderation purposes.

The response form is designed not to request your name or email address. However, information you voluntarily include in an answer may identify you or another person.

Please do not include sensitive personal information or private information about other people in your submissions.

Photobooth

The PROMYS photobooth allows visitors to take photographs through their browser. Photographs are processed temporarily and are not intentionally uploaded to or stored by PROMYS. They are cleared when the photobooth page resets.

Visitors are responsible for saving any photographs they wish to keep before resetting or leaving the page.

Technical information

The website is hosted using GitHub Pages and uses Supabase to store anonymous responses. These providers may process technical information as part of providing, maintaining and securing their services.

The precise technical information processed by these services has not been fully verified. This policy should be updated if further data collection is identified.

03 / How Information Is Used

Submitted responses are used to operate the creative features of PROMYS and may be reviewed by a human for moderation purposes.

Moderation may include identifying content that is inappropriate, harmful, abusive or inconsistent with the website's rules.

We do not intentionally use the anonymous response feature to identify individual visitors.

04 / Storage and Third-Party Services

Anonymous responses are stored using Supabase. The website is hosted using GitHub Pages.

These services may process information under their own privacy policies and service arrangements. Information may be processed in different locations, depending on the services and configurations used.

Their processing of technical information should be considered separately from the information intentionally submitted through PROMYS features.

05 / Data Retention

Submitted responses may remain in the database until they are deleted. A specific retention period has not yet been established.

If you have a concern about a submission or wish to request its removal, please contact us. Because responses are designed to be anonymous, we may need sufficient information to locate the relevant entry.

06 / Data Protection and Your Rights

Where personal data is processed, applicable data protection law may give you rights concerning that information. These may include the right to request access, correction or erasure, or to object to certain processing, depending on the circumstances.

UK data protection requirements may include the UK General Data Protection Regulation and the Data Protection Act 2018.

To raise a privacy concern or make a data-related request, contact us using the details below.

07 / Children's Privacy

Visitors should avoid submitting sensitive personal information about themselves or others through the anonymous response feature.

If you believe that personal information has been submitted inappropriately, please contact us so the matter can be reviewed.

08 / Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated when the website, its features or applicable legal requirements change. The latest version will be published on this page, together with an updated revision date.

We encourage visitors to review this page periodically.
